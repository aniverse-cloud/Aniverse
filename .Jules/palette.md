## 2026-07-12 - Compose Ripple & Touch Targets\n**Learning:** When using Jetpack Compose, `Icon` with a `.clickable` modifier fails accessibility standards by lacking a minimum 48dp touch target and producing an unconstrained, rectangular ripple. Wrapping icons in `IconButton` automatically resolves both issues. Furthermore, explicitly applying a `.clip(CircleShape)` modifier *before* `.clickable` restricts ripple bleed on custom rounded/circular composables, ensuring a polished native UX feel.\n**Action:** Audit all clickable non-standard icons and buttons; consistently prefer `IconButton` or explicit clipping for custom circular touch targets.
